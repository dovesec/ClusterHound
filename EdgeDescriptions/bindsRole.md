# bindsRole

## Edge Schema

- **Source:** [RoleBinding](../NodeDescriptions/RoleBinding.md) / [ClusterRoleBinding](../NodeDescriptions/ClusterRoleBinding.md)
- **Target:** [Role](../NodeDescriptions/Role.md) / [ClusterRole](../NodeDescriptions/ClusterRole.md)

## General Information

The Binding grants the permissions defined in the target Role or ClusterRole to its listed subjects, forming the structural link between a binding and the role it references.
