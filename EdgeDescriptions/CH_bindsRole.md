# CH_bindsRole

## Edge Schema

- **Source:** [CH_RoleBinding](../NodeDescriptions/CH_RoleBinding.md) / [CH_ClusterRoleBinding](../NodeDescriptions/CH_ClusterRoleBinding.md)
- **Target:** [CH_Role](../NodeDescriptions/CH_Role.md) / [CH_ClusterRole](../NodeDescriptions/CH_ClusterRole.md)

## General Information

The Binding grants the permissions defined in the target Role or ClusterRole to its listed subjects, forming the structural link between a binding and the role it references.
